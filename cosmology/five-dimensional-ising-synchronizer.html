Scope

This browser experiment uses five coupled 20×20 lattices, periodic within each layer and open along the axial chain. It is an interactive stand-in, not the essay's full four-dimensional-spacetime-plus-axial lattice. Finite size, interlayer coupling, short equilibration, and single-spin-flip Metropolis dynamics shift and broaden the apparent transition. The measured complexity peak is therefore a finite-run result, not a claim that it occurs at the isolated 2D Ising critical temperature.
