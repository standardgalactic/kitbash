% ==== aniara-seq1-housing-record.md : Ordinary Rounds ====
\seqtitle{Ordinary Rounds}
\trans{FADE IN:}
\sceneheading{INT. ARCHIVE STATION — HABITATION DECK — DAY}
\action{Close on a hand moving across a worn control surface — not sleek, not futuristic-clean. Polished by decades of the same motion. Fingers know it without looking.}
\action{Pull back to reveal SELA (30s), archive technician, mid-shift, three tickets deep in a queue that never empties. She works with the unhurried competence of someone who has done this so many times the job has stopped requiring her full attention — and the specific, plain confidence of someone who knows exactly how a system is supposed to feel.}
\action{A soft chime. Ticket resolves. She doesn't watch it finish — she's already moving to the next.}
\charcue{SELA}
\pparen{not looking up, to no one}
\diag{There you go.}
\action{She glances at ticket two. Something in her posture changes — not alarm, just a small recalibration, the way you'd shift your grip on a doorknob that stuck yesterday.}
\charcue{SELA (CONT'D)}
\diag{Okay.}
\sceneheading{INT. HABITATION CORRIDOR — DECK 14 — CONTINUOUS}
\action{A door. Ordinary. Number plate: 14-C-009.}
\action{A MAN (60s, TAM) stands in front of it, arms crossed, radiating the specific patience of someone who has already complained once and is deciding whether to complain again.}
\action{Sela arrives, satchel of tools she will not need slung over one shoulder — this is, on its face, a software problem, and they both know it.}
\charcue{TAM}
\diag{It's not letting me in.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{Since when?}
\charcue{TAM}
\diag{Since this morning. I went to get bread, came back, it's asking me who I am.}
\charcue{SELA}
\pparen{already interfacing with the panel beside the door}
\diag{It's not asking who you are. It's asking who's supposed to be here. Different question.}
\charcue{TAM}
\diag{Feels the same from out here.}
\action{She works. We're close on her face now — this is the first time we really look at her. Focused, dry, unbothered. A woman who finds other people's panic mildly funny and never says so.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{When did the reallocation go through — the one that moved the Ostrenko family off this deck?}
\charcue{TAM}
\diag{Month ago? Five weeks?}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{And you've lived here how long?}
\charcue{TAM}
\diag{Eleven years.}
\charcue{SELA}
\pparen{mostly to herself}
\diag{So it's not confused about now. It's confused about which now.}
\charcue{TAM}
\diag{Is that bad?}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{It's Tuesday.}
\action{He doesn't find this reassuring. She's not entirely joking.}
\sceneheading{INT. 14-C-009 — INTERIOR VIEW (ARCHIVE OVERLAY) — CONTINUOUS}
\action{We drop, briefly, into Sela's working view — not a dramatic HUD, nothing alien. Just the ordinary texture of her job: a record resolving itself in front of her. Layers of habitation history for this address, stacking, aligning.}
\action{Everything checks out. Origin allocation. The reassignment five weeks ago. Tam's residency, confirmed, timestamped, witnessed by two maintenance visits and one water-use log that couldn't belong to anyone else.}
\action{And yet — layered underneath, faint, contradictory — a second claim. Also complete. Also confirmed. Also perfectly internally consistent. A woman's name we don't catch. A different weight of water use. A different Tuesday.}
\charcue{SELA (V.O.)}
\pparen{quiet, to herself, not to Tam}
\diag{Where did you come from.}
\action{She's not asking the man in the hallway. She's asking the record.}
\sceneheading{INT. HABITATION CORRIDOR — DECK 14 — CONTINUOUS}
\action{Sela steps back from the panel. Something has resolved. Tam watches her face for the verdict the way people watch weather.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{You're you. It's fixed.}
\charcue{TAM}
\diag{That's it?}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{That's it.}
\action{The door, behind him, chimes softly and opens on its own, as if slightly embarrassed.}
\charcue{TAM}
\pparen{already losing interest, already halfway inside}
\diag{Thank you.}
\action{He goes in. Door closes. Ordinary sound of an ordinary door.}
\action{Sela remains a beat too long, looking at the closed door — not worried. Just running something back.}
\action{Down the corridor, at the far end where it bends out of sight, a figure crosses — there and gone in under a second. Dirt on her forearms. Merit's particular unhurried walk.}
\action{Sela almost lifts a hand. Doesn't.}
\action{It's nothing. Merit works three decks from here. Sela doesn't think about it again — not consciously, not yet — and neither, for now, should we. It plays exactly as long as a glance, and no longer.}
\sceneheading{INT. ARCHIVE STATION — MOMENTS LATER}
\action{Back at her station. She's not filing the ticket yet. She's sitting with it.}
\action{A COLLEAGUE, DEV (40s, dry, unbothered by anything, ever) passes with a cup of something hot, glances at her screen in passing.}
\charcue{DEV}
\diag{Ghost record?}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{Sort of.}
\charcue{DEV}
\diag{Reallocation ones always throw a shadow for a week or two. System's just slow to let go of the last tenant.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{It wasn't the last tenant.}
\charcue{DEV}
\pparen{not really listening, already walking away}
\diag{Then the one before that.}
\charcue{SELA}
\pparen{to herself, once he's gone}
\diag{Maybe.}
\action{She looks at the resolved ticket a moment longer. Everything about it was correct. That's the part that's bothering her, though she couldn't yet say why in a sentence that would mean anything to Dev, or to anyone.}
\action{She was expecting a fight. A contradiction that pushed back. Two claims arguing with each other long enough that she'd have to actually decide between them.}
\action{Instead: it simply agreed with itself. Smoothly. Immediately. Like water finding the lowest point without ever having to choose a direction.}
\action{She flags the ticket — not urgent, not escalated. Just: watch. A private note, not a report.}
\action{She moves to the next ticket in the queue. The queue does not care. It never does.}
\trans{CUT TO:}
\sceneheading{INT. HYDROPONIC FOREST — LATER}
\action{Green light. Real light, or close enough — filtered, angled, warm in a way the corridors aren't. Trees that have been here longer than anyone currently alive.}
\action{Sela walks a path she clearly knows without looking at it. Not sightseeing. A shortcut she's taken ten thousand times.}
\action{A WOMAN (30s, MERIT, dirt on her forearms to the elbow) is kneeling by a low bed of something small and green, unremarkable, fussing over it with the specific tenderness people usually reserve for something that isn't a plant.}
\action{Sela slows without quite stopping.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{Still alive?}
\charcue{MERIT}
\pparen{not looking up}
\diag{Barely. Always barely. That's the whole trick of it.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{Command still wants it gone?}
\charcue{MERIT}
\diag{Command wants efficiency. This isn't efficient. It's just —}
\pparen{finally looks up, half a smile}
\action{— it's just from somewhere. That still means something to me, even if it doesn't mean anything on a spec sheet.}
\action{Sela crouches, looks at it. Nothing special to look at.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{What is it, again?}
\charcue{MERIT}
\diag{You've asked me this four times.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{And I'll ask again next month.}
\charcue{MERIT}
\pparen{standing, wiping hands on her knees}
\diag{That's the nicest thing anyone's said to me all week.}
\action{Sela laughs — the first real, unguarded sound from her in the sequence. This is a friendship with history. We don't need to be told that; we can hear it.}
\action{They fall into step together, walking on.}
\sceneheading{INT. FABRICATION MESS — CONTINUOUS (LATER)}
\action{Louder. Metal underfoot instead of soil. A communal eating space bolted directly onto the edge of a working shop — the smell of hot metal and food sharing the same air.}
\action{Sela and Merit sit across from a MAN (50s, HENZO, fabrication, forearms like the machinery he runs) mid-meal.}
\charcue{HENZO}
\pparen{gesturing with a fork}
\diag{— so I tell them, you can't recycle the manifold housing AND keep the backup intact, pick one, and they pick both, like wanting it doesn't change the mass.}
\charcue{MERIT}
\diag{Did they pick?}
\charcue{HENZO}
\diag{Eventually. Badly. Everyone always picks badly and then acts surprised.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{Comforting.}
\charcue{HENZO}
\pparen{shrugs, entirely sincere}
\diag{It's not a complaint. It's a description.}
\action{A beat. Children's voices, distant, sing-song, drift through from somewhere down the corridor — a classroom, doors open for the ventilation. We catch fragments, overlapping with the table conversation, never foregrounded:}
\charcue{CHILDREN (O.S.)}
\pparen{reciting, singsong, half-bored}
\diag{...boundary, maintain an inside... cycle, recover what is used... differentiation...}
\charcue{HENZO}
\pparen{not even glancing that way, mouth full}
\diag{Every year, that generation.}
\charcue{MERIT}
\diag{Every year, every generation.}
\action{Nobody comments further. It's wallpaper to them. To us, it's the first time we've heard the doctrine stated plainly — and it arrives as background noise, competing with lunch.}
\sceneheading{INT. COMMAND ANTEROOM — LATER}
\action{Quieter here. More light, better maintained, the hush of a room used to being taken seriously.}
\action{Sela waits at a low counter with a document slate. Through an open doorway behind the counter we glimpse COMMAND proper — softly lit instruments, a few people at quiet work, nothing dramatic.}
\action{THE NAVIGATOR (70s, upright, precise without being stiff) enters from that inner room, unhurried. He carries himself like a man who has never once been late and never once been in a hurry, which are not the same thing.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\diag{Archive?}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{Signature. Routine.}
\action{He takes the slate without sitting, reads it properly rather than skimming — the way a man reads something he intends to be accountable for.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\pparen{as he reads}
\diag{Reallocation records again?}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{Deck fourteen. Nothing exciting.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\pparen{a small, dry almost-smile}
\diag{Nothing ever is, until it is.}
\action{He signs. Hands the slate back. For a moment he looks at her — not searching, just looking, the way you'd look at someone you've watched do a job for years without ever quite deciding what you think of them yet.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR (CONT'D)}
\diag{How's the queue?}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{Endless. As advertised.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\diag{Good.}
\action{He turns to go. On a side wall, unremarkable, half in shadow, a small display: a stylized star field, a marked point, a number — the destination. No push-in. No held frame. Merit crosses underneath it a moment later, arms full of something from the forest, on her way somewhere else entirely, and doesn't look up at it either. The camera happens to be pointed that way. That's all.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR (CONT'D)}
\pparen{already leaving, half over his shoulder}
\diag{Tell Merit her weed is still winning.}
\charcue{MERIT (O.S.)}
\pparen{from down the corridor, without missing a beat}
\diag{It's not a weed!}
\action{He's already gone. The exchange cost him nothing. It's clearly not the first time he's made the joke, and clearly not the last.}
\sceneheading{INT. ARCHIVE STATION — LATER}
\action{Back where we started. Sela at her station, the day nearly spent. The queue, true to the navigator's word, has not emptied and will not.}
\action{She logs the deck-fourteen ticket properly now — routine, closed, no flag visible to anyone but her. Her earlier private note sits underneath it, unresolved, unescalated. Watch.}
\action{She pauses for exactly one beat before moving to the next ticket — not doubt, not yet. Just the small, private attentiveness of someone filing something away rather than putting it down.}
\action{Then she moves on. The queue does not care. It never does.}
\trans{FADE OUT.}


% ==== aniara-seq2-the-chair.md : The Chair ====
\seqtitle{The Chair}
\sceneheading{INT. MERIT'S QUARTERS — HABITATION DECK — EVENING}
\action{Small, warm, lived-in. Plants on every surface that will hold one, most of them cuttings, none of them precious. Merit is on the floor, cross-legged, holding a chair leg that is very obviously not the same wood as the rest of the chair.}
\action{Sela leans in the doorway, arms crossed, watching this like it's the most normal thing in the world. Because it is.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{You said "quick fix."}
\charcue{MERIT}
\pparen{not looking up}
\diag{This is quick. I'm almost done being wrong about it.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{You said that an hour ago too.}
\action{Henzo is there as well, sprawled sideways across an actual functioning chair, eating something out of a tin, entirely unbothered by any of this.}
\charcue{HENZO}
\diag{Throw it out. I'll bring you a new one Tuesday. Better one.}
\charcue{MERIT}
\diag{It's not about whether it's better.}
\charcue{HENZO}
\diag{It's a chair.}
\charcue{MERIT}
\diag{It's my grandmother's chair.}
\charcue{HENZO}
\pparen{gesturing with the fork, counting}
\diag{Leg's from repair stock. Back rail's from repair stock. Seat's been recovered — what, three times? Four?}
\charcue{MERIT}
\diag{Five.}
\charcue{HENZO}
\diag{So what's left of your grandmother's chair?}
\charcue{MERIT}
\pparen{finally looking up, entirely serious, entirely unbothered by the question}
\diag{The part that's still the chair.}
\charcue{HENZO}
\pparen{to Sela, mouth full, delighted}
\diag{Do you hear this?}
\charcue{SELA}
\pparen{dry}
\diag{I live here too, Henzo.}
\charcue{MERIT}
\pparen{back to the leg, muttering}
\diag{It's not a hard question. You keep replacing what breaks. It's still the same thing. Nobody looks at their own hands and worries they're not the hands they were born with.}
\charcue{HENZO}
\diag{Hands grow. That's different.}
\charcue{MERIT}
\diag{Is it?}
\action{Henzo opens his mouth. Closes it. Genuinely doesn't have an answer ready, which delights him more than having one would.}
\charcue{HENZO}
\pparen{giving up, pointing the fork at her}
\diag{You're insufferable.}
\charcue{MERIT}
\pparen{sweetly}
\diag{I know.}
\action{Sela, still in the doorway, watching the two of them, laughing despite herself — not at the argument, at how completely neither of them notices they're having it. This isn't a scene about continuation. It's two friends being annoying at each other over a chair, and it will stay exactly that size for the whole film.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{I have to go.}
\charcue{MERIT}
\pparen{not looking up}
\diag{You always have to go.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{Someone has to keep the archive from collapsing.}
\charcue{MERIT}
\pparen{finally glancing over, warm, teasing}
\diag{It's fine without you for one evening. It was fine for two hundred years before you were born.}
\action{Sela doesn't have a comeback for that. She just smiles, grabs her jacket, goes.}
\sceneheading{INT. HABITATION DECK — MAIN CONCOURSE — CONTINUOUS}
\action{Sela cuts across the deck's central open space — usually busy at this hour, market stalls, people crossing in every direction.}
\action{Tonight it's emptying fast. Shutters coming down on a food stall. A woman folding a table in on itself with practiced speed. The overhead lighting shifts half a register cooler, almost imperceptibly.}
\action{A MAINTENANCE WORKER passes Sela going the other way, nods at her, keeps moving with purpose.}
\charcue{MAINTENANCE WORKER}
\pparen{not stopping}
\diag{Fifteen minutes to shift change, if you're staying.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{Not staying.}
\action{She isn't being told anything alarming. This is just what the deck does at this hour — a room that belongs to a use, changing hands to the next use, the way it does every night of her life and every night of everyone else's.}
\action{She takes her usual route out. Doesn't look back at the deck emptying behind her. No reason to.}
\sceneheading{INT. SPINE JUNCTION — LIFT BANK — CONTINUOUS}
\action{Three lifts. Sela heads for the middle one out of habit — then, without breaking stride, redirects to the one on the left instead.}
\action{DEV (from Sequence I, passing the other way with an empty cup) clocks this and can't resist.}
\charcue{DEV}
\diag{You skipped the good one again.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{It smells like something died in it.}
\charcue{DEV}
\diag{Maintenance says there's nothing wrong with it.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{Maintenance is wrong.}
\charcue{DEV}
\diag{Maintenance is never wrong. That's the whole problem with Maintenance.}
\action{He laughs at his own joke, more than it deserves, and keeps going. Sela steps into the left-hand lift instead — perfectly fine, unremarkable, smells like nothing at all.}
\action{Nobody in the history of Aniara has ever found anything wrong with the middle lift. People still don't take it. Nobody remembers exactly when that started. It will never come up again.}
\action{The doors close.}
\trans{FADE OUT.}


% ==== aniara-seq3-second-merit.md : The Second Merit ====
\seqtitle{The Second Merit}
\sceneheading{INT. SPINE CORRIDOR — WATER STRATUM ACCESS — NIGHT-CYCLE}
\action{Quieter hour. Lighting dimmed to the ship's version of late. Sela is walking fast, tired, a data slate under one arm — she's been at this longer than her shift technically allows.}
\action{Ahead, through a cross-junction, she catches a figure moving along the perpendicular corridor. Same walk. Same dirt-to-the- elbow economy of motion.}
\charcue{SELA}
\pparen{calling out, not stopping}
\diag{Merit — hey —}
\action{The figure doesn't turn. Keeps walking. Gone around the bend before Sela reaches the junction.}
\action{Sela stands there a second. Looks down the empty corridor. Not alarmed yet — mildly stung, the specific small hurt of being ignored by a friend.}
\charcue{SELA (CONT'D)}
\pparen{to herself, half a laugh}
\diag{Rude.}
\action{She keeps moving toward her own stop.}
\sceneheading{INT. WATER STRATUM — GALLERY LANDING — CONTINUOUS}
\action{Sela comes down a short stair onto the gallery above the circulation channels — the low light, the sound of moving water that has, for years, meant nothing more to her than white noise she likes.}
\action{Merit is already there. Sitting on the rail edge, legs hanging, turning something small over in her hands. She's been here a while — a half-eaten something beside her, cold.}
\charcue{MERIT}
\pparen{not looking up}
\diag{You're late.}
\charcue{SELA}
\pparen{stopped dead in the doorway}
\diag{...How long have you been sitting here?}
\charcue{MERIT}
\diag{Twenty minutes? Since my shift ended. Why?}
\action{Sela doesn't answer right away. She's doing arithmetic she doesn't want to be doing — the corridor, the junction, the walk down here. There isn't enough time in it. There was never enough time in it.}
\charcue{MERIT (CONT'D)}
\pparen{finally looking up, reading her face}
\diag{What.}
\charcue{SELA}
\pparen{too casual, failing at it}
\diag{Nothing. I thought I saw you. Upstairs. A minute ago.}
\charcue{MERIT}
\pparen{shrugs, entirely unbothered}
\diag{Wasn't me.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{I know. I'm — yeah. I know.}
\action{She sits beside Merit anyway, close, the water sound underneath them both. She doesn't say anything else about it. Neither do we. The scene simply continues being an ordinary evening between two people who are tired and glad to see each other — except that for a few seconds Sela's hand, resting on the rail, is gripping it slightly harder than it needs to.}
\action{Merit doesn't notice. Or doesn't say if she does.}
\charcue{MERIT (CONT'D)}
\pparen{back to turning the small object over in her hands}
\diag{Command sent another notice about the bed. Said the water draw's inefficient for the yield.}
\charcue{SELA}
\pparen{grateful for the subject change, taking it fully}
\diag{What'd you tell them?}
\charcue{MERIT}
\diag{That the yield isn't the point.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{That'll go well.}
\charcue{MERIT}
\diag{It never does.}
\action{She almost smiles. Sela almost does too. For a moment the earlier corridor is simply gone from the scene — not resolved, not forgotten, just set down, the way a person sets down something they intend to pick back up later and don't quite manage to.}
\action{The water keeps moving beneath them, exactly the rhythm it has always kept.}
\trans{FADE OUT.}


% ==== aniara-seq4-corrections-dont-converge.md : The Corrections Don't Converge ====
\seqtitle{The Corrections Don't Converge}
\sceneheading{INT. OPTICAL DECK — SMALL OBSERVATION BAY — NIGHT-CYCLE}
\action{A cramped, unglamorous room off the edge of Command, easy to miss if you didn't know it was here. An old optical instrument dominates it — heavy, well-kept, clearly maintained by someone who loves maintaining things. Star field beyond the port, unhurried, indifferent.}
\action{THE NAVIGATOR sits at the eyepiece, exactly as we've seen him before. Two logs open beside him — one automated, one handwritten, in a hand that has clearly been doing this for decades.}
\action{Sela enters without knocking. She's not here for a signature this time. She's carrying a slate, and she's carrying it the way you carry something you've been rehearsing how to say.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\pparen{not turning around}
\diag{You don't usually come up here.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{I don't usually have a reason to.}
\action{He finishes a reading. Writes it in the handwritten log first. Only then does he turn.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\diag{Go on, then.}
\action{She sets the slate down. He doesn't reach for it immediately.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{The correction from eleven days ago. The one Command logged as routine.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\diag{I remember it.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{I pulled the ten before it. And the ten before those.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\pparen{a beat, something in him going very still and very careful}
\diag{And?}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{They converge on a course. A real one. I can show you the math, but you already know the math.}
\pparen{beat}
\action{They don't converge on the destination.}
\action{He doesn't look at the slate. He looks at her for a long moment — not surprised. Something closer to recognizing weather he's seen come in before.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\diag{No.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{Then we're not going there.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\diag{I didn't say we were.}
\charcue{SELA}
\pparen{the ground briefly not where she expected it}
\diag{You— how long have you—}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\pparen{mild, not unkind}
\diag{Longer than you've been alive to ask me that question.}
\action{She doesn't have anywhere to put that yet. She sits, uninvited, on the stool at the edge of the room — not because he offered it, because her legs have decided for her.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{Why do you need us to?}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\pparen{quiet correction}
\diag{I asked you that.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{Then why do you.}
\action{He turns back to the instrument. Doesn't take a reading. Just rests a hand on it, the way you'd rest a hand on something that had earned it.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\diag{I wrote a report once. Younger than you. Better handwriting.}
\pparen{small, dry, almost to himself}
\action{I was very proud of the math.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{What happened to it?}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\diag{Nothing happened to it. That's the part nobody believes when I tell them. Nobody buried it. Nobody called me in and told me to be quiet. My superior read it. Checked it. Agreed with every figure.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{And then?}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\diag{And then I asked her what we were going to tell people.}
\pparen{beat}
\action{She asked me what, exactly.}
\action{Sela opens her mouth. Doesn't have the sentence yet.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR (CONT'D)}
\diag{Go on. Finish it for her. What were we going to tell them.}
\charcue{SELA}
\pparen{confident, immediate}
\diag{Tell everyone we're not going to arrive.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\diag{And then?}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{We—}
\action{She stops. Not because he's stopped her. Because she's heard what the next word would need to hold up, and it doesn't.}
\action{He doesn't wait for her to find it. Doesn't smile, doesn't press. Just goes on, already past it.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR (CONT'D)}
\diag{That's forty years, right there, in your face right now. I've had considerably longer to sit with it and I'm no closer to an answer than you are to a sentence.}
\charcue{SELA}
\pparen{recovering, and not conceding anything}
\diag{That's not the same as a reason to do nothing.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\diag{I didn't do nothing.}
\pparen{gestures faintly at the instrument, the two logs}
\action{I did this. Every day. Same as you're about to start doing, whether you decide to or not.}
\action{He finally picks up her slate. Reads the correction figures properly — the way he reads everything, like a man intending to be accountable for it.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR (CONT'D)}
\diag{This is good work.}
\charcue{SELA}
\diag{People built lives on that destination. Somebody should have known.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR}
\pparen{handing the slate back, not unkindly}
\diag{I know. That part doesn't go away either.}
\action{He returns to the eyepiece. Takes a reading — the ordinary, practiced motion. Logs it in the automated system. Then, in the handwritten log, beside it, exactly as always.}
\action{For a moment, pen still on the page, he pauses — something in the figure catching him sideways. Looks at it a second longer than the motion usually takes.}
\charcue{NAVIGATOR (CONT'D)}
\pparen{quiet, mostly to the instrument}
\diag{Huh.}
\action{Sela glances over. He doesn't offer anything. A beat — then he finishes the line, unbothered, and moves on to the next reading.}
\action{Sela doesn't ask. Neither do we.}
\trans{FADE OUT.}


% ==== aniara-seq5-hibernation-interlude.md : Hibernation ====

\action{No alarm. No light change anywhere on the hull. No mechanism reaching toward it, retracting from it, sealing around it. The ship does not appear to know, or to care, or to have decided anything about what is happening to it.}

\action{Distance increases. The growth's edge — where hull becomes growth becomes hull again — was never sharply defined even up close. At this distance it stops being resolvable as an edge at all.}
\action{We keep pulling back.}
\action{There is an interval — we could not say how long, and the film does not mark it — during which the boundary in question simply passes below whatever threshold of resolution the shot can register. This is not withheld from us. It was never available to be shown.}

\action{Aniara — whale-shaped, dark, continuing along its unhurried line through the field, entirely unchanged in bearing, unchanged in course, giving no indication that anything about it is different from the ship we have watched all film.}
\action{And, separate from it now, something smaller.}
\action{We hold on it as it rotates, slowly, against the star field.}
\action{From one angle: a fragment. Torn, irregular, an edge that could be damage.}
\action{The rotation continues.}
\action{Along one face, a curvature persists — the same interval, the same seam-spacing we have seen in the hull's own layered plating all film. Nothing about that face contradicts where it came from.}
\action{The rotation continues.}
\action{Elsewhere on the same form: a continuous, unbroken curve running through where a joint should be — exactly the kind of transition Fabrication's modular units, established throughout this film, cannot produce without a seam. Nobody aboard Aniara builds anything that way.}
\action{The rotation continues. Both properties remain true of the same object at once. No fourth angle arrives to settle which one matters more.}
